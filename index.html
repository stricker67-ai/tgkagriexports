Trusted Coconut Oil Manufacturing & Bulk Supply from Akola

Tirupati Oil Corporation (TOC) is a registered partnership firm headquartered at Plot No. J 62, Phase III MIDC, Akola, Maharashtra (PIN 444104), with our wholesale trading office at G-12, J. K. Traders, Wholesale Kirana Market, Khamgaon Road, Akola (PIN 444001).

Led by partners Hitendra Jairamdas Gurbani and Madhur Hitendra Gurbani, we crush, filter, and pack high-purity coconut oil from selected sun-dried and milling-grade copra for edible retail packs, food processors, cosmetic formulators, and export buyers.

Every batch undergoes strict moisture, free fatty acid (FFA), and clarity checks to ensure natural aroma, long shelf life, and zero chemical adulteration.

TOC Pure Edible Coconut Oil

Traditionally expeller-pressed and double-filtered from selected milling copra. Rich natural roasted coconut aroma, zero argemone or mineral oil, packed in pouches, jars, and 15 kg tins.

HS 1513.19

Virgin Coconut Oil (Cold-Pressed)

Cold-pressed from fresh matured coconut kernel without chemical refining. Retains natural lauric acid (48–52%), antioxidants, and delicate aroma for health food, retail, and personal care.

🧴
HS 1513.19

Refined Coconut Oil (RBD)

Refined, bleached, and deodorized coconut oil with neutral odor, water-white clarity, and low FFA (<0.1%). Ideal for confectionery, bakery, commercial frying, and oleochemical users.

HS 1513.11

Crude / Bulk Copra Oil

Unrefined expeller coconut oil supplied in 190 kg HDPE/MS drums, IBC totes, and flexi-tanks for soap manufacturers, hair-oil blenders, and refineries.

HS 2306.50

Copra Expeller Cake & Meal

Nutrient-rich coconut oil cake (20–22% protein, 8–12% residual oil rich in lauric acid) from our expeller pressing line, supplied in 50 kg PP bags for cattle and dairy feed.
